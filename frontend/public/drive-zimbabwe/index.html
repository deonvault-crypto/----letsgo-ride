Driver FAQ

Questions drivers ask.

How much commission does LetsGoRide take from a driver's ride fare?

LetsGoRide takes 0% commission from the driver's ride fare. Other payment-provider or banking charges, where applicable, are separate from LetsGoRide commission.

How do I join LetsGoRide as a driver?

Download LetsGoRide, choose the driver journey and complete the required profile and verification steps shown in the app. Approval depends on meeting the applicable onboarding requirements.

Does LetsGoRide guarantee a specific amount of driver earnings?

No. LetsGoRide does not guarantee a specific amount of earnings. Driver activity and fare income depend on completed rides, demand, availability and other operating factors.
