Harare ride FAQ

Questions about riding with LetsGoRide.

How do I book a ride in Harare with LetsGoRide?

Open LetsGoRide, set your pickup and destination, choose an available ride option and send your request. Availability depends on active drivers and service coverage.

Is LetsGoRide available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. LetsGoRide is available on the Apple App Store and Google Play. Visit letsgoride.site/download to reach the correct store.

Is LetsGoRide an e-hailing app for Harare?

LetsGoRide is a Zimbabwe-focused mobility platform that supports ride requests in Harare where service is active, alongside other services in the LetsGoRide app.

Does LetsGoRide take commission from a driver's ride fare?

LetsGoRide does not take a percentage commission from the driver's ride fare. Other payment-provider or banking charges, where applicable, are separate from LetsGoRide commission.
