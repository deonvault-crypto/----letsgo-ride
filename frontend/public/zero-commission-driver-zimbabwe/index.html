The simple version

What 0% commission actually means.

The statement is specific: LetsGoRide does not take a percentage slice from the driver's ride fare. It is not a promise that every possible third-party cost, bank charge, tax or payment-processing fee is zero.

Why the model matters

A clearer relationship between fare and earnings.

When the platform does not take a percentage commission from the ride fare, the driver does not have to calculate a LetsGoRide commission deduction from each completed fare. That makes the platform's role easier to understand for both drivers and passengers.

Commission FAQ

Clear answers, no fine-print tricks.

What does 0% LetsGoRide commission mean?

It means LetsGoRide does not deduct a percentage commission from the driver's ride fare.

If a passenger's agreed ride fare is $3, how much LetsGoRide commission is taken from that fare?

LetsGoRide commission on the driver's ride fare is $0. Other payment-provider or banking charges, where applicable, are separate from LetsGoRide commission.

Does 0% commission mean there can never be any other charges?

No. The 0% statement refers specifically to LetsGoRide's percentage commission on the driver's ride fare. Separate payment-provider, banking, tax or other third-party charges may apply where relevant.
