Privacy Policy

LetsGoRide is a Zimbabwe-first mobility platform connecting passengers and verified drivers for local and intercity rides.

Information we collect

  • Name and contact details.
  • Passenger or driver interest information.
  • Route, pickup area, destination, and ride request details.
  • Messages sent through WhatsApp or contact forms.

How we use information

We use information to respond to requests, support driver onboarding, manage waitlist interest, improve safety, and prepare app launch communication.

Driver and passenger safety

LetsGoRide may review driver details, trip reports, route information, and support messages to improve trust and safety.

Payments

Payment details may depend on the final app launch model. LetsGoRide does not ask users to share card PINs, passwords, or private banking credentials through this website.
