Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 August 2026 · Contact: support@letsgoride.site

This policy explains what personal data LetsGoRide handles across rides, food, courier, driver, courier-partner and merchant features, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Scope and who we are

LetsGoRide is a Zimbabwe-focused mobility and local-services platform. It supports customer, driver, courier and merchant experiences. The iOS app is published under the LetsGoRide listing by Wisewave Sp. z o.o.; the service is operated under the LetsGoRide brand. In this policy, “LetsGoRide”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the LetsGoRide service.

This policy applies to the LetsGoRide mobile apps, website, support channels and related platform services.

2. Data we collect

Depending on your role and the features you use, this can include account and contact data; authentication and security data; pickup, drop-off and other location data; ride, food and courier activity; driver, courier and merchant verification data; support and safety records; device and notification data; and payment-related amounts, statuses and transaction references when a payment feature is used.

3. How we use data

We use data to create and secure accounts, provide rides, food, courier, merchant and partner workflows, coordinate users where a service requires it, display route and delivery information, review partner eligibility, send service and security messages, provide support, investigate safety matters, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain platform reliability and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Location information

LetsGoRide can request location access when location is needed for features such as selecting your current pickup point, choosing a precise handoff location, or supporting an active ride or delivery. You can deny location access and use manual location selection where the product allows it.

We do not intend to track your location continuously when no location-dependent service is active.

5. Identity and verification documents

Driver, courier and merchant roles may require additional verification. Verification documents are sensitive and are not intended for public display. Access is restricted to authorised review and support workflows and protected delivery paths.

6. When information is shared

We may share limited information with another user when necessary to complete a confirmed service, for example pickup, destination, name or contact information needed to coordinate a trip or delivery. Private verification documents are not made publicly available.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the platform, to investigate fraud or safety incidents, or as part of a lawful corporate transaction.

7. Service providers

LetsGoRide relies on specialist providers for infrastructure such as cloud hosting, databases, media and document storage, email delivery, maps and geocoding, push notifications, security and app distribution. These providers process data for the services they supply to LetsGoRide and under their own contractual and security obligations.

8. Sale of personal data and targeted advertising

LetsGoRide does not sell personal data. The app is not designed around behavioural advertising or the sale of user profiles to advertisers.

9. Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, to operate active accounts, resolve support and safety matters, protect the platform, meet accounting or legal requirements, and handle disputes or fraud prevention. When information is no longer needed, we delete it, de-identify it, or restrict it from ordinary product use where continued retention is legally or operationally necessary.

10. Account deletion

If you created a LetsGoRide account, you can initiate account deletion from Account → Delete Account in the app, or use our external account-deletion page.

External deletion request: https://letsgoride.site/delete-account

Deletion removes the account and associated personal data that LetsGoRide no longer needs. Limited records may be retained where required for legal obligations, safety, fraud prevention, disputes, accounting or security.

11. Security

We use technical and organisational controls intended to protect personal data, including authenticated access, role-based authorisation, server-side session controls, password hashing, restricted document access, validation of uploaded files, rate limiting and security logging. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Your privacy choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing of your personal data, or to request a copy of information associated with your account. Some verified identity fields are intentionally not self-editable after review and may require support-assisted correction and re-verification.

13. International processing

LetsGoRide users may be in Zimbabwe while service providers or infrastructure may operate in other countries. Where personal data is processed across borders, we use appropriate contractual, security and access controls required by applicable law.

14. Younger users

LetsGoRide is not designed as a service for children who cannot legally enter into the relevant transport, delivery or commercial arrangements. Where local law requires parental or guardian consent for a younger user, the service should be used only with the required permission and supervision.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, law or our data practices change. The current version and effective date will remain available at this URL.

16. Contact

Privacy, account and data requests can be sent to support@letsgoride.site.
