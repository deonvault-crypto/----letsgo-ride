Harare ride guide 🇿🇼

How to book a ride in Harare.

If you are looking for a simple way to request a ride in Harare, LetsGoRide keeps the flow clear: choose your pickup, add your destination, review what is available and send your request.

Step by step

From opening the app to sending your request.

01

Open LetsGoRide

Use the LetsGoRide app on iPhone or Android.

02

Set your pickup

Choose the point in Harare where you want the driver to meet you.

03

Add your destination

Enter where you are going and review the ride options currently shown in the app.

04

Send your request

Request the ride and follow the trip details when an eligible driver is available.

Before you request

A few things that make pickup easier.

Use a clear pickup point

Choose a place that is easy for both you and the driver to identify. Confirm the pin before sending the request.

Check your destination

Make sure the destination shown in the app matches where you actually want to go before you request the ride.

Review what the app shows

Ride options and availability can vary. Use the current information shown in LetsGoRide rather than assuming a driver is always available.

Keep trip information in one place

Use the in-app journey details to follow the request and trip flow instead of relying on scattered information.
