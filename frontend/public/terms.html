LetsGoRide

Terms of Use

LetsGoRide helps passengers and drivers connect for ride sharing across Zimbabwe. The website promotes the service and collects interest before full app launch.

How LetsGoRide works

  • Passengers request routes or join the waitlist.
  • Drivers register interest and prepare for onboarding.
  • The app will support clearer route information, driver profiles, booking records, and support flows.

User responsibility

Users must provide accurate information and must not misuse the service, submit fake requests, or impersonate another person.

Driver responsibility

Drivers must follow road laws, maintain vehicle safety, communicate honestly, and respect passengers.

No guarantee

Website information can change before full app launch. Ride availability depends on location, route demand, driver onboarding, and operational readiness.
