Harare airport rides 🇿🇼

Your airport ride, from the same app.

Heading to or from Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport? Use LetsGoRide to set your pickup and destination and request an available ride where service is active.

Airport travel without a separate booking flow.

Use the normal LetsGoRide ride-request experience for airport journeys. Enter a suitable pickup point, add the airport or your destination in Harare, review the available ride options and request your trip.

Choose where you want the driver to meet you. At the airport, follow any local pickup instructions or designated access areas that apply.

Airport access, pickup points, traffic conditions and driver availability can change. LetsGoRide does not guarantee a specific pickup time or airport availability.

Airport ride FAQ

Before you travel.

Can I request a LetsGoRide trip to Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport?

You can enter the airport as your destination and request a ride where LetsGoRide service and an eligible driver are available.

Can I request a ride from Harare airport after I land?

You can open LetsGoRide after arrival, set an appropriate airport pickup point and request a ride where service is active. Driver availability can vary.

Does LetsGoRide guarantee airport pickup times?

No. Pickup timing depends on active drivers, traffic, service coverage and the pickup location you select.

Flying in or heading out?

Download LetsGoRide and request your Harare airport ride when service is available.
