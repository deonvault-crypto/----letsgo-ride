Before requesting

A few details make pickup easier.

Check your pickup pointUse a location where the driver can reasonably meet you and where vehicle access is permitted.
Confirm your destinationMake sure the destination shown in the app matches where you want to go before requesting.
Allow for real-world conditionsTraffic, active-driver supply and service coverage can affect availability and pickup timing.
Keep trip details in the appUse the LetsGoRide journey information shown for your ride rather than relying on assumptions about timing or availability.
