For drivers

The Ride Now fare belongs to the driver.

LetsGoRide takes 0% platform commission from Ride Now fares. The driver keeps 100% of that fare. That is a product decision, not a launch-week gimmick.

Drivers still decide when to work and which eligible requests to take. Account and vehicle requirements still apply.
