Trust by design

Useful controls, not empty badges.

LetsGoRide uses role checks, account verification workflows, private document handling, secure sessions and clear support routes to reduce avoidable risk.

Private verification records

Identity and verification documents are not intended for public display and are handled through protected access paths.

Account integrity

Reviewed driver, courier and merchant identity details cannot be casually rewritten through normal profile editing.

Permission-aware

Location, notifications, camera and photo access are requested only through the app when the related feature needs them.
