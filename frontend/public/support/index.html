Before you message us

Send the detail that helps.

Include the email on your LetsGoRide account, the relevant ride/order/delivery reference if you have one, and a short explanation. Never send your password or one-time verification code.

Account changes

Reviewed driver, courier and merchant identity changes may require support review and re-verification.

Safety reports

Describe what happened and include relevant trip/order/delivery details. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Privacy requests

Use your account email where possible so we can verify that the request belongs to you.
