Support

Get help with your LetsGoRide account, rides, verification, and safety questions.

Effective date: 23 May 2026

1. Contact support

LetsGoRide support can help with your account, rides, verification, and safety questions. We aim to review support messages as soon as possible. Response times may vary.

Email support@letsgoride.site

2. What we can help with

  • Account access.
  • Email verification.
  • Password reset.
  • Profile details.
  • Phone number updates.
  • Ride requests.
  • Driver trip posting.
  • Driver verification.
  • Support messages.
  • Safety reports.
  • Account deletion requests.
  • Privacy questions.

3. Safety and urgent issues

LetsGoRide support is not an emergency service. For immediate danger, road accidents, threats, or crime, contact local emergency services or law enforcement first. After you are safe, you can also report the issue to LetsGoRide support.

4. Driver verification support

Drivers can contact support if documents were rejected, they need to update files, or they need help understanding verification requirements.

5. Account deletion

Users can request deletion inside the app or contact support. Some records may be retained for safety, fraud prevention, dispute handling, legal, or operational reasons.

6. Privacy support

Users can contact support for questions about personal data, phone privacy, verification documents, account records, and deletion requests.

7. Before contacting support

To help us review your request, include your account email, route or ride details if relevant, screenshots if helpful, and a short description of the issue.

Do not send passwords or one-time verification codes to support.
