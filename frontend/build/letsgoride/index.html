How It Works

Getting started with LetsGoRide is simple and takes less than 2 minutes.

  1. 1. Create an Account

    Register for free using your email address or phone number. Verify your identity for added trust.

  2. 2. Search for a Ride

    Enter your departure city, destination, and travel date to browse available rides instantly.

  3. 3. Book & Pay Securely

    Reserve your seat with a secure payment. Receive instant confirmation via SMS and email.

  4. 4. Enjoy the Ride

    Meet your driver at the agreed pick-up point and travel comfortably to your destination.

About LetsGoRide Zimbabwe

LetsGoRide is Zimbabwe's leading carpooling platform, connecting travellers with verified drivers for shared rides across the country. Founded in Harare in 2022, our mission is to make inter-city travel more affordable, reliable, and environmentally sustainable.

We rigorously verify every driver on our platform — checking licences, vehicle roadworthiness, and criminal records — so you can travel with confidence. Our community-driven rating system ensures consistent quality on every journey.

  • Verified drivers & vehicles
  • Reduce your carbon footprint
  • Save up to 60% vs. bus fares
  • 24/7 customer support
